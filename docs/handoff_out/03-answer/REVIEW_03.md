## Their numbers, reproduced or not

Every number of hand-off 03 that these data can test was reproduced: the clean set (409 joins, 389 kept, rank 6, robust sd 0.87 µm), the six foils to 0.1 µm, the half-year agreement to 0.5–0.9 µm (the second half alone does not determine foils 4 and 5), F = 2.2–2.3 at the 25/26 → 20/22/23 joins with S1 0.04 → 0.10 mm (other gap changes give other F: table in check 1, so 'every time' holds per gap change), Monc/Monp ×1.55 to 1.62 in the anomalous series (×1.69 in one R20 join) with residuals +54 ± 1 µm, R04/R05 at about +11 µm (+10.6, +11.5 µm for their joins with S1 changed), and R18's S1-only joins at ratio 0.91 and −19 to −21 µm. One join in R10 (2026-06-28) is anomalous on its own (+164 µm, F 6.5): a failed join, not the series.

## What this means for the user's statement

The user said that Monc normalises S1 changes. In most runs it does: across the S1 0.04 → 0.10 mm change Monc rises ×2.0–2.3 and the detector ×2.1–2.3, residual within a few µm. From 2026-09-26 (R17 to R21) and, by about 10 µm, in R04/R05, it does not: Monc rises only ×1.55–1.62 while the detector rises ×2.3. Reflectivity patched across those joins is unaffected (the patch measures the step); anything that uses Monc to compare scans at different S1 gaps without patching is off by up to 46 % in those runs. Measured.
