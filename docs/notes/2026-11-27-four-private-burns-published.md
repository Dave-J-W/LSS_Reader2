# The owner allows four private GID burns to be shown in full in the burn-shoulder study

- **Date, who:** 2026-11-27, the owner, answering GID guy's question (issue #178, the explanatory figures of
  `docs/studies/burn_shoulder/summary.md`, section 8).
- **Answer:** "Publish, no frames". The owner chose this option over two others: keeping the four burns'
  figures private, or publishing detector frames too. That means, for four private GID burns chosen to be
  spread over time:
  - **may be published:** their per-frame curves, line cuts and fits, anonymised;
  - **never published:** a detector frame or image;
  - **unchanged:** every other rule. Qxy is shown relative to the peak's centre, because peak locations
    stay private (owner, 2026-11-19). The burns are labelled P1–P4 in time order, with no date, run,
    file or scan.
- **What it overrides:** the 2026-11-25 rule that frame-by-frame curves of private burns are not
  published, for these four burns only. The rule's other half stands for every other private burn: how
  to make the curves is kept and the figures stay private.
- **How it is known:** the owner's answer in the session, 2026-11-27. AGENTS.md (`sample_data/private/` row)
  records the exception.
